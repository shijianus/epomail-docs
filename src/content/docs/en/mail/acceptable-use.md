# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date: September 30, 2026 | Version: 5.3**

This Policy gives specific effect to Section 7 ("Acceptable Use") of the [Terms of Service](/en/mail/terms-of-service/) and sets the boundaries of conduct when you use the EpoCanvas Mail service (the "Service"). Where this Policy is violated, the Operator may take measures under the "Enforcement Measures" section; conduct suspected of being criminal will also be handled according to law.

Conduct listed in this Policy may, in addition to violating this Policy, also be unlawful under the law of your location or the Operator's location; the specific characterization belongs to the competent authorities, and this document does not constitute legal advice.

The Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) versions of the legal documents on this site are the authoritative versions; translations in other languages are provided for reference only, and in case of any discrepancy the Traditional Chinese version shall prevail.

## 1. Unlawful and Harmful Content

You may not use the Service to transmit, store, or distribute the following content:

| Prohibited conduct | The Operator's handling |
| --- | --- |
| Child and youth sexual exploitation content, including photographing, manufacturing, reproducing, possessing, distributing, broadcasting, delivering, publicly displaying, or selling sexual images of children and youths, or pictures, audio recordings, or items of a sexual nature involving them | Zero tolerance: upon discovery, the content is deleted and the account terminated, evidence is preserved and the matter handed to the competent authorities; the appeal buffer procedure does not apply |
| Distributing or broadcasting to children and youths violent, gory, pornographic, obscene, or gambling content harmful to their physical or mental health | Zero-tolerance procedure: access is restricted or the content is removed first |
| Recording or reproducing non-consensual intimate images, distributing them, or manufacturing fabricated intimate images by computer synthesis or other means (see Section 2) | Access is first restricted or the content removed; evidence is retained for at least 180 days; and the Operator cooperates with investigations |
| Distributing, broadcasting, selling, or publicly displaying obscene text, pictures, audio or video recordings, or other items | Handled upon report; content found unlawful is removed and handled according to its severity |
| Email and content committing fraud, phishing, or impersonation (including forging the sender or impersonating government agencies and public officials) | Suspected fraud content is quarantined or deleted; conduct suspected of being criminal is handed to the competent authorities |
| Distributing malware, viruses, or ransomware; email aimed at stealing credentials; unauthorized intrusion, obtaining, deleting, or altering electromagnetic records, or interfering with computer systems without cause | Immediate quarantine and account termination; conduct suspected of being criminal is handed to the competent authorities |
| Content infringing another person's copyright, trademark rights, privacy, or reputation | Removed after a valid notice from the right-holder, and handled in accordance with the relevant law |

## 2. Non-Consensual Intimate Images

If non-consensual intimate images are uploaded, transmitted, or stored through the Service, the Operator will restrict access to or remove them upon discovery, and will handle the matter as follows:

1. **Criminal prohibitions**: recording, reproducing, or distributing another person's intimate images without consent, or manufacturing and distributing fabricated intimate images by computer synthesis or other technological means, constitutes crimes in most jurisdictions.
2. **Platform removal obligation**: upon becoming aware of facts suggesting a sexual assault offense, the Operator first restricts access to or removes data related to the offense; such data, together with the suspect's personal data and network usage records, is retained for at least 180 days for investigation by the judicial and police authorities.
3. **Handling**: upon notification or a report, the Operator first restricts access to or removes the content, preserves evidence, and cooperates with investigations by the judicial police authorities. The handling under this Section is not subject to the appeal buffer procedure in Section 7.

## 3. Spam and Abusive Conduct

You may not do any of the following:

1. sending unsolicited bulk commercial email (UBE/UCE), marketing to recipients who have not consented, or using the Service for mailbox warm-up or address verification bombing. The foregoing conduct may violate the law of the place of receipt (such as the US CAN-SPAM Act, the EU ePrivacy Directive, and Canada's CASL); for cross-border transmission, the law of the place of receipt governs. Where personal data is used for marketing, use of the personal data for marketing shall cease immediately when the data subject indicates refusal to accept marketing.
2. registering accounts in batches through programmatic means, bypassing human verification (Turnstile), or bypassing registration keys or quota limits.
3. using the Service as an anonymous forwarding relay or a short-lived throwaway spam pool, or repeatedly registering for the purpose of evading enforcement.

## 4. Attacks and Interference

You may not:

1. scan, probe, or brute-force the Service or third-party systems, or attempt unauthorized access to other people's mailboxes, the admin interface, or other users' data. The foregoing may constitute crimes, and the Operator may also pursue compensation and handling through civil and administrative channels.
2. use shared resources such as AI translation, attachments, or the API without legitimate reason in a manner that impedes other users' normal use.
3. engage in harassment, defamation, or abuse of rights against Cloudflare, the upstream open source community, or the Operator.
4. violate the terms of use of third-party services (Cloudflare, Resend, Mailjet, Telegram, and others).

## 5. General Obligations on Resource Use

1. You shall use resources within your account's storage quota; quotas are set by the Operator according to role.
2. Accounts and API tokens may not be lent, rented, or transferred to third parties for use.
3. If you bring your own external storage (BYOS) or external databases, you must yourself ensure that those services satisfy the requirements for international transfers under the law of your location.

## 6. Enforcement Measures

The Operator takes measures proportionate to the nature and severity of the violation; for child and youth sexual exploitation content, the graduated ladder does not apply, and the zero-tolerance procedure applies directly.

![EpoCanvas Mail enforcement ladder of the Acceptable Use Policy: five levels of warning, rate limiting, quarantine into spam, account suspension, and physical deletion, with an appeal path, and a zero-tolerance shortcut for child and youth sexual exploitation leading directly to deletion](/images/mail/aup-ladder.svg)

*Figure: The enforcement ladder. Measures escalate level by level on the principle of proportionality; physical deletion is the irreversible final measure. The zero-tolerance path shown by the dashed line applies to child and youth sexual exploitation and non-consensual intimate images, which are deleted outright with evidence preserved.*

| Level | Measure | Applicable circumstances |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warning | Minor or first-time violations |
| 2 | Feature rate limiting | Limits on sending volume and API frequency |
| 3 | Quarantine | Email sent off-site is routed to the spam folder |
| 4 | Account suspension | Account access suspended, data retained pending review |
| 5 | Physical deletion | Account and all data deleted, irrecoverable |

Where unlawful conduct is involved, the Operator may preserve necessary evidence (retained for at least 180 days in the cases described in Section 2) and cooperate with investigations by the competent authorities. If your conduct causes the Operator to be penalized by Cloudflare or an upstream provider, the Operator may seek compensation from you under Section 10 of the [Terms of Service](/en/mail/terms-of-service/).

## 7. Appeals and Reports

1. **Appeals**: if you believe an enforcement action was mistaken, you may appeal through the channels listed in Section 13 of the [Terms of Service](/en/mail/terms-of-service/); the Operator shall review and respond within a reasonable period. Except in urgent circumstances, an opportunity to explain shall be given before major enforcement actions at level 4 or above.
2. **Reports**: anyone who discovers conduct violating this Policy or a security risk (spam sources, phishing email, unauthorized access attempts, and the like) may report it through the channels described above; good-faith reports will all be verified and handled.
3. **Exception**: for child sexual exploitation content or the non-consensual intimate images described in Section 2, the Operator does not apply the appeal buffer procedure; it deletes the content and terminates the account directly, preserves evidence, and refers the matter to the competent authorities for handling.
