## 1. Unlawful and Harmful Content

Do not use the service for the following conduct. Decisions must consider reliable information, actual harm and possible lawful uses; urgent isolation and statutory reporting follow Sections 6 and 7.

| Prohibited conduct | Operator's response |
| --- | --- |
| Producing, possessing or distributing child sexual abuse or exploitation material, or grooming children for abuse | Immediately restrict access and stop distribution; preserve necessary evidence and report as required by law, and review account termination |
| Providing children with violent, sexual or gambling content that the law prohibits providing to them | Restrict harmful access first; remove content or restrict accounts according to law and severity |
| Creating or distributing intimate imagery without consent, including harmful synthetic sexual imagery | Promptly restrict access or remove; retention and appeals follow Section 2 |
| Obscene content whose distribution, sale or public display is unlawful | Verify notices and applicable law, then take necessary action |
| Fraud, phishing, impersonation or sender forgery used to deceive others | Quarantine content, restrict affected functions and act according to law |
| Malware distribution, credential theft, unauthorised intrusion or system damage | Immediately isolate harm and review; lawful isolated testing under Section 4 is assessed within its authorisation |
| Copyright, trademark or privacy infringement, or unlawful disclosure of others' data | Verify rights and grounds for lawful use, following Section 8 or the applicable procedure |

## 2. Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery

Using the service to create, transmit or distribute intimate imagery without consent is prohibited. On a credible notice sufficient to locate the material, the operator should promptly restrict access or remove it to reduce further dissemination.

1. Assess removal, evidence preservation and reporting duties under the law actually applicable, without treating one jurisdiction's rules as universal.
2. Retain evidence for at least 180 days only where applicable law expressly requires it, following that law's scope, covered persons and starting point. Otherwise retain it for the shortest lawful and necessary period. Isolate evidence, restrict access to authorised personnel who need it, and clear it on expiry unless another lawful retention ground exists.
3. Disclosure to supervisory or law-enforcement authorities requires a legal basis, verification of the recipient and a necessary scope. Urgent removal does not remove later appeal rights; reasons may be withheld or delayed as legally permitted where disclosure is prohibited or would prejudice an investigation.

## 3. Spam and Abuse

The following conduct is prohibited:

1. Sending unsolicited bulk mail, marketing to recipients who have not consented, mailbox warm-up or address-verification bombardment. In addition to this policy, comply with applicable sending-location, receiving-location and other anti-spam rules; stop marketing when rejected.
2. Unauthorised bulk account registration or bypassing Turnstile, registration keys or quotas.
3. Creating abusive relays or temporary account pools, or repeatedly registering to evade enforcement.

## 4. Attacks and Interference

Learning, debugging and security testing are permitted on a deployment you control that is isolated from real services, using synthetic data or anonymous data assessed as not allowing reasonable re-identification of individuals, and ensuring third parties are not affected. Merely replacing names, masking addresses or hashing identifiers does not automatically meet the standard for anonymity; see Section 4.5 of the [Privacy Policy](/en/mail/privacy-policy/) for the scope of assessment. Testing does not authorise access to real mail, personal data or other people's credentials.

1. Before scanning, load testing, social engineering, brute-force testing or access testing against public hosted instances, real-user systems or third-party systems, obtain explicit authorisation from the system's responsible party and comply with its scope, time limits and stop conditions. Public accessibility is not authorisation.
2. Do not deliberately exhaust shared AI, attachment or API resources or interfere with others' use; authorised capacity tests must stay within agreed limits.
3. Good-faith criticism, vulnerability reports, privacy complaints, reports to authorities and lawful exercise of rights are not abuse. Threats, targeted harassment and harmful disclosure of private information are prohibited.
4. Third-party terms apply only within the services actually used and relevant contractual relationships; they do not transfer the operator's own duties to users.

## 5. General Obligations on Resource Use

1. Follow the instance's published storage and sending quotas; changes are notified under the Terms of Service.
2. Do not rent out or resell accounts or give credentials to unauthorised parties. Lawful integrations should use limited authorisation and protect keys.
3. Before using BYOS or an external database, check access permissions and processing conditions. Users and the operator fulfil their respective actual roles; a user's choice does not exempt the operator from data-protection and international-transfer duties.

## 6. Enforcement Measures

Measures must be proportionate to the nature, reliability of evidence, harm and repetition of the breach. Child abuse, non-consensual imagery or an ongoing attack may be isolated or disabled without waiting through each step; subsequent review still applies.

![Levels of warning, rate limiting, isolation, suspension and termination, with an appeal route](/images/mail/en/aup-ladder.svg)

*Figure: levels are selected according to circumstances; urgent protection and subsequent appeals coexist, while deletion and evidence retention follow law and technical limitations.*

| Level | Measure | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warning | Minor, first-time breaches that can be corrected |
| 2 | Rate limiting | Limiting sending volume, API frequency or affected functions |
| 3 | Isolation | Restricting access or delivery to prevent further harm |
| 4 | Account suspension | Pausing access and lawfully retaining necessary data for review |
| 5 | Termination and deletion | Serious or repeated breaches; deletion, retrieval and lawful retention follow the Privacy Policy |

See [Data Processing & Security](/en/mail/data-security/) and the [Privacy Policy](/en/mail/privacy-policy/) for quotas, scheduled cleanup and backup limits. Account suspension does not immediately clear all copies; deletion does not guarantee that data in every system is unrecoverable.

Statutory content reports follow applicable law and disclose only necessary data to authorised recipients. Content is not automatically shared with all third parties, upstream providers or reporters. Users are responsible only for conduct legally attributable to them, without a blanket duty to reimburse all operator or developer costs, fines or third-party claims.

## 7. Appeals and Reports

1. If a decision appears wrong, appeal through `admin@epocanvas.com` or Terms Section 13, including when you cannot log in. Normally, reasons and a chance to respond should precede major action; review and reply should follow within a reasonable period.
2. Reports should give necessary location details, timing and reasons, without redistributing victim imagery, passwords or unrelated personal data. The operator prioritises by credibility and harm; statutory privacy-request deadlines are governed separately by the Privacy Policy.
3. Urgent measures may precede notice but do not cancel subsequent appeal or access to courts and authorities. Errors found on review should be corrected as far as law and technical feasibility allow; restoration of deleted data cannot be promised.

## 8. Copyright and Intellectual Property Notice and Counter-Notice

Rights holders may provide specific details of the right and alleged infringement, enough information to locate the content, contact details and legally required statements. The operator should verify the notice, authorisation and applicable exceptions or limitations before restricting access, removing material or requesting further information. A unilateral allegation does not itself establish infringement.

Affected users may provide a counter-notice and grounds for lawful use. Notices, counter-notices and identity details are forwarded only to the extent necessary under an applicable procedure and legal basis, with notice as required by law, not automatically sent in full to the reporter. Repeated infringement or deliberately false notices are addressed according to facts, proportionality and law; the internal procedure does not replace statutory remedies.
